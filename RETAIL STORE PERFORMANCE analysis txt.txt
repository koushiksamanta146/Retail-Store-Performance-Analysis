RETAIL STORE PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD

Business Context :
The objective is to understand how different stores, regions, product categories and price segments contribute to sales and profitability, and identify opportunities to improve overall retail performance.

Business Objective :
Identify key revenue and profitability drivers and optimize store, product and regional performance for sustainable growth.

Business Problem :
Management needs to identify underperforming locations, low-margin categories, regional demand patterns and store productivity gaps to make better decisions on inventory allocation, pricing, promotions and expansion.

Business Question :
Q1. Location Performance Analysis – Revenue, Units Sold & Profit					
Q2. Location-wise Basket Value, Basket Size, No. of Transactions					
Q3. Which region contributes the highest sales and profit?
Q4. Which price segments (Budget, Mid-Range, Premium) drive the highest revenue?
Q5. Monthly Sales Trend by Location
Q6. Category-wise Sales Contribution by Location
Q7. Product Category Comparison by Profit Margin, Units Sold, and Sales
Q8. Month-on-Month Sales Change by Region
Q9. Average Sales per Store by region
