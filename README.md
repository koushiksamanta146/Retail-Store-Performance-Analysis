# Retail-Store-Performance-Analysis
Excel-based retail sales analysis to identify revenue, profitability, regional, category and store performance drivers.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail store performance across **locations, regions, product categories, price segments, and time periods**.

The analysis was performed on a **100K transaction-level retail dataset** to identify key revenue and profitability drivers, understand performance gaps, and provide actionable recommendations for management.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

**Identify key revenue and profitability drivers and optimize store, product, and regional performance for sustainable growth.**

---

## 💼 Business Problem

Management needs to identify the key drivers of sales and profitability, understand regional and store-level performance gaps, and determine where **pricing, assortment, inventory, and promotional strategies** should be optimized.

---

## 🏢 Business Context

The dataset represents a multi-store retail business operating across multiple cities and regions, with products across categories such as **Writing, Office, Art, and School**.

The dataset contains transaction-level sales information along with supporting data related to **stores, products, customers, footfall, targets, and inventory**.

The analysis focuses on answering key business questions around:

* Which locations and regions drive revenue and profit?
* Which product categories drive sales versus profitability?
* Which price segments contribute most to revenue?
* How does average transaction value vary by location?
* Which regions have higher store productivity?
* How does sales performance change month-to-month?

## 📈 Analysis & Key Insights

### 1. Region-wise MoM Sales Changes

Regional sales show significant month-to-month volatility, with **West recording the strongest December growth (+18.4%)** and South also showing strong growth (+8.6%).

**Management Action:**
Align inventory and promotions with high-growth periods to reduce sales volatility.

---

### 2. Product Price Segment Performance

The **₹0–500 budget segment generates ₹132.2M**, contributing approximately **64% of total sales** and significantly outperforming higher price segments.

**Management Action:**
Protect budget-segment availability while driving upselling to higher-value products.

---

### 3. Sales & Profit by Region

**South leads on profit (₹24.9Cr, 37% margin); West is smallest by sales but has the best margin (43%); East has decent sales but the weakest margin (18%).**

**Management Action:**
Scale South, replicate West’s margin strategy, and improve East’s profitability.

---

### 4. Category Sales Contribution by Region & Category Profit Margin

Each category is primarily driven by one region — **School (East), Writing (North), Art (South), and Office (West)** — while profitability varies sharply across categories. **Art has the highest margin (39%)**, while School has the lowest (21%).

**Management Action:**
Localize inventory by regional demand while prioritizing high-margin categories.

---

### 5. Average Sales per Store by Region

**North stores are the most productive per store (₹26.7L), while East stores are the least productive (₹17.3L), highlighting a clear store-level efficiency gap.**

**Management Action:**
Benchmark North’s productivity and improve East’s store-level efficiency before expansion.

---

### 6. Location Performance

**Jaipur is the strongest-performing location, leading both sales (₹28.6M) and profit (₹8.8M), while Delhi records the lowest sales (₹12.9M).**

**Management Action:**
Scale Jaipur’s success model while addressing Delhi’s demand and assortment gaps.

---

## 💡 Key Business Takeaways

* **South** is a major profit contributor and should remain a priority growth region.
* **West** demonstrates strong margin efficiency despite lower overall sales.
* The **₹0–500 price segment** is the primary revenue driver.
* **Writing** is a strong volume driver, while **Art** provides stronger profitability.
* Regional category preferences indicate the need for **localized assortment planning**.
* **North** demonstrates strong store-level productivity.
* **Jaipur** is the strongest individual location based on sales and profit.
* Lower-performing regions and locations present opportunities for **pricing, assortment, inventory, and operational optimization**.

## 🚀 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, management should focus on:

1. **Regional Inventory Optimization**
   Align inventory with region-specific category demand.

2. **Margin Improvement**
   Review pricing and cost structures in low-margin regions and categories.

3. **Basket Value Growth**
   Use cross-selling and upselling to increase average transaction value.

4. **Store Productivity**
   Benchmark high-performing stores and replicate their operating practices.

5. **Seasonal Planning**
   Use historical sales trends to align inventory and promotions with demand peaks.
